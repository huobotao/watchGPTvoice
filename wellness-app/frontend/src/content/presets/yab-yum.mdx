## Yab-yum — kinesiology notes

**Loaded chains.** A's adductors, internal hip rotators, and erectors all
hold isometric length; B's hip flexors and adductors do the same plus a
small co-contraction in the gluteals to keep the pelvis stacked over A's.
Energetically near-zero — almost all "work" is breathing and gentle pelvic
rocking.

**Pelvic alignment.** B's pelvis sits in A's lap, both pelvises in deep
flexion (~90–100°) and abducted ~40°. The contact axis is vertical;
because both spines are upright, the diaphragms align — used in tantric
practice for paced breathing as a coregulation tool.

**Range-of-motion prerequisites.**
- Hip external rotation ≥ 35° (otherwise the knees rise off the floor and
  the medial knee takes torque).
- Adductor length sufficient for sitting cross-legged (Sukhasana) without
  posterior pelvic tilt.

**Common faults.**
- A's lumbar collapses into flexion → places B's weight on a flexed disc.
  Stack ribs over pelvis; consider a cushion under A's sit bones.
- Knees rise above the hips → reduce hip abduction (bring legs in) and
  prop the knees on cushions.
- B grips with adductors against A's torso → adductor cramp; let B's
  weight settle through gravity instead.

**Skip / modify when.** Knee meniscus injury, hip impingement (FAI), late
pregnancy (deep flexion + supine return).
