## Spooning (side-lying, same direction) — kinesiology notes

**Loaded chains.** Effectively zero — this is the lowest-effort geometry in
the library. Both partners' weight is borne by the lateral hip + ribcage
through the support surface; muscular activity is limited to small postural
adjustments.

**Pelvic alignment.** Pelvises are stacked laterally with a small
posterior offset (B behind A). Hip flexion ~40–55° on both sides folds the
knees forward, which automatically tilts the pelvises ~10° anteriorly and
brings the contact axis into a usable angle without any extensor work.

**Common faults.**
- B's lower shoulder takes A's full weight → rest A's neck on a pillow,
  not on B's bicep.
- A's lower arm gets trapped under the ribcage → tuck it forward (shoulder
  protraction) instead of under.
- B's upper hip rolls forward, twisting the lumbar spine → keep both
  hips stacked, or use a pillow between the knees to maintain stack.

**Indications.** Post-surgical recovery, late-pregnancy (no supine vena-cava
compression), partners with major joint pain, conditions where high heart
rate is contraindicated.
