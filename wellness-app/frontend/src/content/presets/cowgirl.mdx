## Cowgirl — kinesiology notes

**Loaded chains.** Partner B's quadriceps and gluteals do the rhythmic work
through repeated mini-squats (~25° knee-flexion oscillation around a 110°
baseline). Partner A is mostly passive but holds an isometric pelvic floor
contraction to stabilise the contact axis.

**Pelvic alignment.** With B's pelvis stacked vertically on A's, axial
loading is straight downward — the cleanest geometry for B-led depth and
angle control. Small forward / backward lean of B's trunk (lumbar ±10°)
changes anterior-wall pressure without changing depth.

**Common faults.**
- B "bounces" with knees rather than hips → quadriceps fatigue in 60 s and
  patellofemoral compression rises. Drive from the gluteals instead.
- A grips B's hips and pulls down → axial overload on A's lumbar; let A
  support B's thighs laterally instead.
- B leans back far enough to load the wrists on A's knees → wrist
  hyperextension. Use forearms on A's thighs.

**Skip / modify when.** B has acute knee pain (especially patellar
tendinitis), or A has rib fractures / chest-wall pain — B's hands often
brace on A's chest.
