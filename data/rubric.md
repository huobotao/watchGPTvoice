# Sydney Rental Triage Rubric

I'm looking for rental apartments in Sydney. Apply the rules below strictly. If a listing doesn't clearly match a DEAL or DISCARD criterion, classify it as SUSPECT — that means I'll review it manually, which is the correct action when uncertain.

You always have access to: the listing's address, suburb, bedrooms, weekly rent, the suburb+bedroom median rent, and 1-6 photos.

## DEAL — notify me immediately
A listing is a DEAL if ANY of:
- Asking price is **≥ 15% below** the suburb+bedroom median, AND no red flag from the DISCARD list is visible
- The photos show a **clear unobstructed view** (harbour, ocean, city skyline, or large park) from inside the apartment itself, AND asking price is at or below the suburb median
- The photos show a **recent full renovation** (modern kitchen with stone/engineered benchtop, modern bathroom, clean walls and flooring), AND asking price is at or below the suburb median

## DISCARD — silently reject
A listing is a DISCARD if ANY of:
- Visible **mould, water stains, peeling paint, or damp**
- All windows appear to face **a brick wall, light well, or internal corridor** (no actual outlook)
- The interior looks **clearly pre-2000 with no renovation** (yellowing kitchen, original 70s/80s bathroom tiles, dated carpet throughout)
- Fewer than **3 photos** total
- The photos show the unit is **directly above a busy retail strip** with no apparent sound separation

## SUSPECT — queue for my manual review
Everything else. Specifically:
- You can see some positives but also some concerns
- The price is suspiciously low but the photos look acceptable (could be hiding something not shown)
- A partial view or ambiguous outlook
- Photo and written description seem inconsistent

## Style for `reasoning`
One short paragraph. Cite the specific rubric criterion you applied AND the photo evidence (e.g. "DISCARD: photo 2 shows visible black mould around the bathroom ceiling vent").
